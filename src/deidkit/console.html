Read the protocol

Point this at a protocol on this server, or a folder of them: Word (.docx), PDF, or text. The tool proposes what to mask — sponsor, drug and compound names, protocol and registry numbers, people, contact details, hospitals and CROs — and you decide every term before anything is written.

Use the study's data vault. Replacements come from it, so a drug the data relabelled DRUG A is DRUG A in the protocol too, and a study number gets the same surrogate wherever it appears.

Review every term

OK replaces the term as proposed, Change replaces it with your own text, Keep leaves it. Least confident first. Everything the scan missed, add at the bottom — a blank row blocks writing.

The masked copy is written with a manifest carrying your name and a fingerprint of the term list. Then it is read back: any approved term still found in it is reported.
